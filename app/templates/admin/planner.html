Planning rules

What the engine guarantees

  • No deliberate overlap with school, tuition, travel or sleep.
  • Completed work is preserved during regeneration.
  • Missed work is reported, not silently discarded.
  • Infeasible workload is flagged instead of hidden.
  • Feasibility is not treated as mastery.

Known limits

Planning is heuristic

Estimates come from syllabus metadata and student preferences. The planner does not know actual comprehension, school holidays or sudden schedule changes; students must reschedule and use recovery honestly.
