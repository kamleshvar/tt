Ranking logic

What moves first?

  1. Higher-priority and high-weightage work.
  2. Earlier missed deadlines.
  3. Work that fits without overlapping school, tuition, travel, sleep or other tasks.
  4. At most two recovered tasks per day where capacity allows.
